Our Mission

The Pain Management Support Group (PMSG) was first implemented at the East Dallas Memorial Community Hospital. PMSG serves asa safe, supportive environment where individuals living with chronic pain can connect, share experiences, and learn effective coping strategies together.

🤝

Peer Support

Connect with others who understand your journey and share valuable insights from lived experience.

📚

Educational Resources

Access evidence-based information about pain management techniques and treatment options.

💪

Coping Strategies

Learn practical tools and techniques to manage pain and improve quality of life.

🏥

Professional Guidance

Benefit from facilitation by healthcare professionals specializing in pain management.
