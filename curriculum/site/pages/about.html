About Our Support Group

Building a community of hope and healing

Our Story

The Pain Management Support Group was founded at East Dallas Memorial Hospital System to address the growing need for comprehensive support services for individuals living with chronic pain.

What We Offer

  • Weekly peer support group meetings
  • Educational workshops on pain management
  • Resource library with evidence-based information
  • One-on-one peer support connections
  • Family and caregiver support sessions
